<Outcome title="Where it landed.">

Poly Punch was a student project and was shelved when the term ended, so it never shipped. It was built on an experimental hand-tracking package, which made it as much an exercise in designing against a moving platform as a game: gestures had to stay readable under load, and the levels had to teach them without a single pop-up breaking presence.

</Outcome>
