# dragoncraft: media folders

Keep each section’s images and videos together. Adding files does not automatically publish them.

- `hero/`: current cover and trailer.
- `gallery/`: general gallery artwork, not feature-section media.

- **Pick a dragon, then grow into it.** — `01-the-fantasy/` (source only; not currently rendered)
- **350 mods that respect your time.** — `02-curation-is-the-design/` (source only; not currently rendered)

Section folders are under `sections/`. Case-study media is connected in `content/portfolio/pages.json`; Sidequest content is connected in `content/projects/dragoncraft.mdx`. Shared images can be referenced from another section without copying them.
