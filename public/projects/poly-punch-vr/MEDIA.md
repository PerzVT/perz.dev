# poly-punch-vr: media folders

Keep each section’s images and videos together. Adding files does not automatically publish them.

- `hero/`: current cover and trailer.
- `gallery/`: general gallery artwork, not feature-section media.

- **Combat gestures, no controllers.** — `01-hand-tracking/` (source only; not currently rendered)
- **Earning new ways to move.** — `02-progression/` (source only; not currently rendered)
- **Levels instead of tutorials.** — `03-teaching/` (source only; not currently rendered)

Section folders are under `sections/`. Case-study media is connected in `content/portfolio/pages.json`; Sidequest content is connected in `content/projects/poly-punch-vr.mdx`. Shared images can be referenced from another section without copying them.
