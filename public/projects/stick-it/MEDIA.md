# stick-it: media folders

Keep each section’s images and videos together. Adding files does not automatically publish them.

- `hero/`: current cover and trailer.
- `gallery/`: general gallery artwork, not feature-section media.

- **Rules the player can read.** — `01-stealth-loop/` (source only; not currently rendered)
- **Patrol, suspicion, pursuit.** — `02-guard-ai/` (source only; not currently rendered)
- **One new tool at a time.** — `03-progression/` (source only; not currently rendered)
- **Seven days, four people.** — `04-running-the-team/` (source only; not currently rendered)

Section folders are under `sections/`. Case-study media is connected in `content/portfolio/pages.json`; Sidequest content is connected in `content/projects/stick-it.mdx`. Shared images can be referenced from another section without copying them.
